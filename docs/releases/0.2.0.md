# Baliset 0.2.0

Covers get a proper home: a folder's own art first, then the file's embedded art, then — only when you ask — MusicBrainz and the
Cover Art Archive. No streaming, no accounts, and still no network traffic unless you turn the cover lookup on.

## What is new

- **Folder covers.** An image named `cover`, `folder`, `front`, `album`, `albumart`, `artwork` or `thumb` (jpg, jpeg, png, webp or
  bmp) beside the music is used for the whole album. This works offline and beats everything else.
- **Find cover art** (album ••• menu): searches MusicBrainz for the album's title and artist, ranks the editions against the track
  count and year, and shows up to four candidates with a preview and a match percentage. Pick one with **Use**; nothing is written
  until you do. The album shows **Cover from MusicBrainz** under the title, and **Reset cover** returns it to the file/folder art.
- **Fill missing covers** (Settings → Album art): a library-wide pass over albums with no cover at all. It only applies matches of
  74% or better, runs one MusicBrainz request a second, shows progress, and can be stopped. The switch is off by default and the
  first use explains exactly what is sent.
- **Cover data** credits, and per-album storage of which release a cover came from (`files/cover-meta.json`).
- The cover reload fix: a newly fetched or reset cover appears immediately, without leaving the album page.

## How the matching works

Title, artist, track count and release year, with the MusicBrainz search score as a small tie-breaker; editions are collapsed by
release group because they share the artwork. A title mismatch is never accepted, and a different track count costs a lot, which is
what keeps a 19-track deluxe edition from being chosen for a 5-track album. Folder art and embedded art always win over a lookup.

## What was tried

On the emulator (1080 × 2400, Android 17 / API 37), with a five-track *Kind of Blue* and a folder-cover album:

- the folder cover was picked up by the scan and shown in the library, Home and the player;
- **Find cover art** matched the album at 94%, previewed the real cover, and wrote it; Home, Library, the album page and the mini
  player all updated without a restart;
- the provenance line and `cover-meta.json` were written, and the candidate sheet asked for consent the first time;
- the Album art settings group, the switch and the fill row rendered and behaved.

**Not tried:** the library-wide **Fill missing covers** against a large real library, a physical phone, and the reset path (the code
is shared with the apply path, but the button was not exercised on the emulator). MusicBrainz returned one transient 503 during
testing; the client retries once after the delay the service asks for, and the sheet's **Try again** worked.

## Installing

Sideload the APK. It is signed with the same key as 0.1.0, so it installs over it. A 0.1.0 library, playlists and settings are kept.
