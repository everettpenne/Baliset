# Baliset 0.3.0

An Apple Music library is mostly AIFF, and neither Android nor ExoPlayer can read it. Baliset 0.3.0 can: tags, cover art, length,
playback and seeking all come straight from the file. No conversion, no duplicate library, no new permission.

## What is new

- **AIFF and AIFC playback.** A small Media3 extractor reads the `FORM`/`COMM`/`SSND` chunks and plays 16-bit linear PCM in both the
  usual big-endian AIFF and little-endian `sowt` AIFC files (which Apple also writes). Big-endian samples are byte-swapped to what
  Android's audio pipeline expects. Constant-bitrate seeking works, and AIFF tracks sit in the queue beside MP3 and the rest.
- **Tags and covers without Android's help.** Android's tag reader has no AIFF support, so Baliset reads the file's own ID3v2.2/2.3/2.4
  tag: title, artist, album, year, track number and the attached front cover. Length comes from the `COMM` frame count and rate.
- **The folder route is the honest one for AIFF.** Android's media index does not list AIFF at all, so **Scan my phone** will not see
  these files. **Choose a folder** does, with no storage permission; the welcome screen and Settings now say so. The index still works
  for everything it can see.
- **Nothing to convert.** The files stay where they are; the extractor reads them in place. A 790-file, 33 GB AIFF library needs no
  cache and no second copy.
- The non-music files Apple keeps beside a library (`.strings`, `.plist`, `.itlp`) are ignored, as before.

## What was tried

The library that prompted this was 919 files: **790 `.aif`**, 1 `.aiff`, 47 `.mp3`, and the rest Apple metadata. Two real AIFF samples
and one MP3 were copied to the emulator (1080 × 2400, Android 17 / API 37):

- the scan read the ID3 tags, cover and length from both the big-endian `.aif` (The Cure) and the `sowt` `.aiff` (Vampire Weekend),
  and the MP3;
- both AIFFs played, the player showed the right format (44.1 kHz stereo), the queue moved to the MP3 and back, and tapping the
  scrubber jumped to 1:52 of a 3:33 track;
- 45 JVM tests pass, including the new ID3v2.2/2.3/2.4 and AIFF chunk tests.

**Not tried:** the whole 33 GB library at once, a physical phone or GrapheneOS device, 24-bit or floating-point AIFF, and compressed
AIFF variants (`ima4`, `alaw`, `ulaw`), which are rejected with a clear message rather than played as noise. WMA, APE, MPC, DSD and
FairPlay `.m4p` remain out of scope; this library had none of them.

## Installing

Sideload the APK. It is signed with the same key as 0.2.0, so it installs over it and keeps the library, playlists and settings.
